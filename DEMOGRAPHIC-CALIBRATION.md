# Demographic Calibration

The country-neutral Human calibration layer translates normalized evidence into deterministic `PopulationCohortInput[]`. It is tooling and initialization content outside frozen `PopulationState`; reports and source metadata are not saved.

## Evidence and packages

Primary evidence classifications are `observed`, `estimated`, `assumed` and `projected`. Derived and calibrated describe output lineage, while missing knowledge is an explicit gap. Source descriptors identify an organisation, publication/dataset and release, reference period, jurisdiction, publication status and methodology. URLs and retrieval dates are metadata, not identity.

A version-1 package fixes its country, exact effective date, universe, birth-year partition, sources, measures, authority, distribution, reconciliation, age conversion and generation-profile bindings. Its versioned ID is an immutable data contract. A canonical, code-point-ordered UTF-8 `fnv1a64-v1` fingerprint detects accidental content mutation under an existing ID; revisions require a new package ID. FNV-1a is a deterministic integrity check, not a cryptographic signature or protection against a deliberate collision. The package registry binds each known ID to its expected fingerprint. Saves retain canonical cohort reality and the originating package ID in `PopulationCoverage.source`.

## Compilation

Source decimals use a bounded canonical unsigned form: noncanonical leading/trailing zeroes, signs, exponents and negative zero are rejected. They become reduced exact rationals with bounded precision. Largest-remainder allocation conserves an integer authority and breaks equal remainders by stable target ID. Completed-age bands may be divided among birth years uniformly by exact Gregorian birth day. This is a disclosed calibration assumption, not evidence of exact birthdays. Rounded broad-band evidence retains both limitations in the report. Open-ended bands block complete coverage until a tail model is explicitly supplied and are never redistributed into supported cells. Living-population partitions cannot contain a birth year after the effective year.

One authority is selected; competing totals are diagnostics and are never averaged. Reconciliation is either exact or proportional within a declared threshold. Universes must match by explicit stable universe ID. A derived authority can establish complete coverage only when every material magnitude leaf is observed or estimated. Assumed allocation inside such a total is permitted and reported; assumed magnitude cannot establish completeness. Projected evidence may remain package metadata for diagnostics, but v1 rejects it when selected to construct starting cohorts.

Demographic `COMPLETE` means exhaustive conserved accounting with no demographic blocking gap. It does not mean every cell was directly observed, and it does not mean naming or intrinsic Person-generation content is production-ready. The compiler exposes demographic coverage and generation readiness separately. Generation-profile resolution remains an operational requirement for Population initialization and cohort-to-Person instantiation. The machine report is authoritative; readable text is rendered from it and distinguishes direct resolution, broader-band allocation and exact canonical counts derived from rounded evidence.

The first country-specific evidence adapter is outside these generic primitives under `src/data/demography/uk/ons-mid-2024/`. It verifies pinned official ONS artifacts by SHA-256 and targets release `MYE24UK` plus the compatible very-old-age CSV. Immutable candidate `uk.population.mid-2024.v1` retains its original gap record and fingerprint. Successor `uk.population.mid-2024.v2` uses `uk-old-age-tail.geometric-adjacent-v1` to close its statistical partition without changing the official population magnitude. The successor is demographically complete but remains generation-not-ready until production Human content resolves its profile binding. See `UK-DEMOGRAPHIC-EVIDENCE.md`.

The old-age model derives exact ratio `933/1516` from pooled adjacent source cells, assigns exact rational weights `r^(age-105)` over ages 105–119, normalizes them to the rounded 105+ evidence weight, and reconciles all rounded ages 90–119 together to the authoritative unrounded 90+ subtotal. Exact output cells are calibrated simulation values, not ONS single-age estimates. Age 120 is only the terminal assumption of this initialization model. Deterministic sensitivity variants remain report diagnostics and never enter PopulationState.

Generic format adapters, temporal interpolation, open-age tail modelling, uncertainty scenarios, Geography validation and historical backtesting remain deferred. Future backtests initialize from immutable package A and compare simulated state with immutable package B; package B never overwrites history.
