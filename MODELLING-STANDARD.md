# Modelling Standard

Turning Pages separates evidence, modelling choices and authored gameplay so exact simulation state is not mistaken for exact knowledge of reality.

## Categories

1. **Hard mechanics and invariants** define accounting, conservation, identity and deterministic transition rules.
2. **Statutory and institutional rules** model published legal or procedural requirements and record their jurisdiction and effective period.
3. **Observed statistical data** reports a direct measurement for a stated population, date and resolution.
4. **Empirical or official estimates** report a published estimate rather than a direct observation.
5. **Calibrated or derived transformations** convert or reconcile disclosed evidence into canonical simulation inputs.
6. **Uncertain behavioural assumptions** are explicit modelling choices whose outcomes are not established facts.
7. **Authored gameplay abstractions** compress reality to make the simulation playable and must not be presented as empirical claims.

Absence is not zero. An estimate is not an observation. A forecast is not history. Calibration is not measurement. Canonical exact integers and dates do not imply that source evidence had the same resolution. Model outcomes must not be selected to validate preferred political, economic or moral conclusions.

Identity-facing generation content follows the same rule. Registration-count weights may be evidence-backed when their lineage supports that claim. A reviewed authored name prior is an authored gameplay abstraction, remains labelled as such, and must never be presented as demographic frequency evidence or used to infer demographic or psychological attributes.

Versioned Geography content must classify boundary and hierarchy sources accurately. A statutory definition, observed statistical geography and authored synthetic/test partition are different evidence categories. Place identity and partition precision do not establish residence, physical presence, citizenship or demographic assignment.

Reality and evidence supply starting conditions. Simulation mechanics produce future history. Record material assumptions and limitations near the model that uses them; do not add universal runtime provenance to every game variable.
